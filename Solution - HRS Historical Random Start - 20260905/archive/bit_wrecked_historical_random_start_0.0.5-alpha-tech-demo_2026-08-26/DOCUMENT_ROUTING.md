## Source-of-Truth Rules

- The manifest defines intended product behavior and hard boundaries.
- Current situational awareness summarizes the active working state but does
  not override the manifest or evidence.
- The plan defines sequence and gates; it does not override the manifest.
- Evidence reports describe what was actually inspected or tested.
- Review/log entries preserve reasoning but do not authorize implementation.
- The UX parity manifest may delegate reversible design judgment but cannot
  override product semantics, write boundaries, runtime gates, or open owner
  decisions.
- Runtime observations outrank assumptions and historical examples.
- A newer dated evidence report may supersede an older technical finding, but
  the conflict must be recorded in the changelog and routed from here.
- Open owner decisions stay visibly open; an AI must not infer acceptance from
  the existence of a proposed design.

## Payload and Publication Boundary

Documentation organization does not authorize gameplay changes. Live game
files, the live `Mods` folder, saves, worlds, Harmony, packaging, installation,
publishing, pushing, uploading, and distribution remain separate explicit
actions.

The root BAT is an approved design-shell entry point, not a runtime mod or
installer. Its active support files are under `Support_Files_Do_Not_Edit/`, and
its empty payload boundary is documented under `Payload_Template/`.

## Purpose

Turn the Historical Random Start development story into an accurate,
sponsor-ready account of human-in-the-loop engineering with AI. This manifest
separates verified technical facts, owner observations, unfinished work, and
public claims so outreach can move quickly without overstating the product.

The story is not that AI generated a game mod by itself. The story is that a
human operator and an AI development partner built a repeatable way to ask the
game what was actually true, convert observations into tests, and preserve the
reasoning for later developers and AI sessions.

## Why the story is valuable

1. QA found a correct DLL whose packaged version identity did not match the
   release notes. That failure produced stronger artifact provenance,
   manifests, handoffs, and evidence routing.
2. A simple two-point spawn idea expanded into full-catalog landing research
   when actual terrain, chunk loading, collision, slopes, POIs, and hazards
   contradicted assumptions.
3. Automated carousel runs increased coverage, while the human operator stayed
   in the loop to recognize failures such as underground placement, malformed
   terrain, unsafe drops, carried scene objects, enemy interference, and
   visually unstable landings.
4. The landing method evolved through priming, terrain resolution, a second
   placement above the resolved surface, grounded observation, and fail-closed
   rejection.
5. Arrival protection became immutable per-character state: only the dangerous
   family belonging to the initial grounded biome may be suppressed.
6. Opening-trader routing evolved from globally nearest to the reviewed trader
   in the player's initial biome so early progression does not require an
   unintended biome crossing.
7. Reload and death testing proved that relocation and trader routing remain
   one-shot. An accidental monster death in the Pine control produced a normal
   respawn and an explicit HRS lifecycle rejection rather than another warp.
8. Documentation is treated as executable continuity: later teams and AI
   sessions can recover not only what changed, but why the method changed.

## Claims not yet allowed

Do not publicly claim any of the following until its named gate closes:

- “Version 1.2.0 is released,” “QA approved,” or “Nexus ready.”
- “Every spawn point is safe” or “works on every generated map.”
- “Only the initial biome is disabled everywhere” until the four-run
  cross-biome isolation matrix completes.
- A precise count of final production spawn points until the release catalog
  and release manifest are sealed together.
- Compatibility with arbitrary player-generated worlds until prefab identity,
  map availability, fallback, and random-world tests pass.
- Any subscriber, download, reach, conversion, revenue, or delivery metric that
  has not been measured and captured.

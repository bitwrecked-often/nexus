## Why 1.0.1 was created

The first `1.0.0` QA Apply attempt stopped with:

`Verified QA payload hash mismatch. Do not install; redownload the QA package.`

The game installation was not the cause. The packaged `ModInfo.xml` had
SHA-256 `A6DF8103F4CDC6923C01904C378016F9ECA1FE982AB40DB9BA3253AB2608D1C2`,
while the manager and release manifest still pinned
`ED14DB4450CB449B3628539BF3D3A9998D9D1709B141D80367C47641C1E5C419`.
Clicking Apply correctly triggered the fail-closed check before installation.

QA preserved `hrs_1.0.0` as the failed candidate and created a separate
`hrs_1.0.1` repair lane. No repair was made in the failed candidate.

## What currently works

- The portable manager starts through `START.bat`.
- The final payload passes its manager hash checks.
- The supported game build is checked before deployment.
- The deployment layer rejects missing, changed, drifted, or conflicting files.
- Standard and Random policy contracts pass their pure tests.
- The runtime contains twelve curated Navezgane survey anchors.
- Relocation remains one-shot, bounded, and fail-closed in the tested models.
- Nearest-trader selection is relational to the relocated player's position
  among the five version-pinned Navezgane trader placements.
- The earlier `HRS_TRADER_006` development proof demonstrated relocation,
  tutorial completion, nearest-trader selection, objective update, and route
  completion. That historical proof was not rerun as a `1.0.1` live test.
- Optional snow protection remains scoped to the relocated NG01 character and
  selected game.

## What is limited, unproven, or intentionally unsupported

- New anchor locations are not validated merely by adding coordinates. Each
  needs ground, headroom, water, bounds, chunk-load, and landing QA.
- Arrival protection is snow-specific. It does not suppress desert, wasteland,
  burnt-forest, weather, temperature, enemy, or ordinary damage hazards.
- The trader implementation is pinned to Navezgane and its five reviewed
  trader placements. Custom worlds are not supported by this proof.
- Multiplayer, dedicated servers, EAC-enabled play, other maps, and game builds
  other than V3.2 (b9) are not proven.
- Apply success does not prove relocation, tutorial progression, trader
  routing, persistence, death behavior, or rollback for this exact binary.
- A consumed character marker must prevent relocation from running again.
- Unsafe or unavailable anchors should fail closed to ordinary game behavior;
  they must not be counted as successful custom starts.
- The manager no longer ships a shortcut. Users must launch `START.bat`.

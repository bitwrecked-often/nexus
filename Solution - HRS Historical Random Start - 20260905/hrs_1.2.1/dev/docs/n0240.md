Date: 2026-09-01

## Purpose

This record defines the evidence boundary for turning the current development
lane into the first Nexus-facing base build. It explains why the packaged
versions differ, identifies the exact production candidate, and separates
release behavior from the QA-only full-catalog carousel.

## Evidence interpretation

The deterministic carousel certifies repeatable geometry and landing health
without zombie interference. God mode prevents damage from masking geometry;
collision remains on and fly/noclip remains off so grounded evidence stays
meaningful. A later smaller normal-enemy sample validates representative player
experience without allowing hostile pushes to invalidate the whole geometry
set.

Human observation remains part of the proof. Telemetry verifies identity,
health, position, restoration, and grounded state; the operator identifies
hazards such as partial burial, hostile overlap, unstable prefab collision,
fence/cliff edges, or unacceptable vertical drops.

## Nexus promotion gate

Do not call or package a Nexus release from this baseline until all of the
following are true:

1. Preserve the completed 171/171 controlled survivor certification and its
   raw event/result files and hashes. **Complete.**
2. Resolve or explicitly exclude every stop; setup failures never reject a POI.
3. Complete fresh ordinary-player NG01 and NG02 production tests without God
   mode, including grounded arrival, unchanged progression, nearest trader,
   starter quest, save/reload, exactly-once behavior, and rollback on failure.
4. Run a bounded normal-enemy experience sample separately from geometry
   certification.
5. Re-run release, manager, artifact-identity, package-inventory, install,
   removal, rollback, and sanitized-log contracts against the exact candidate.
6. Build the Nexus archive only from the exact verified payload and record its
   file inventory and SHA-256. QA tools, arms, bridge files, disposable saves,
   raw logs, and development-only carousel binaries must not enter the player
   archive.

Until those gates pass, this document is a reproducible base-build handoff,
not a publication claim.

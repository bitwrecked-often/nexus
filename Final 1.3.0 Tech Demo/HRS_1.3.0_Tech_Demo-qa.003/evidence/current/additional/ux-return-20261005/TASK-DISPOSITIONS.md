## Fixture incident and corrective evidence

Early attempts are retained. The first focused launch omitted the SmokeTest flag,
so the normal picker read an existing installed policy and the initial assertion
stopped before any Apply, Launch, Uninstall or fixture preparation. Subsequent
staging asserts its exact inert game root.

A later harness removed its temporary inventory-reader override without restoring
the imported reader. The product correctly moved into unverified state; the driver
then failed an assertion while leaving its shown form alive after its PowerShell
pipeline ended. The resulting NoRunspace dialog and red-X paint were fixture
teardown artifacts. The harness now preserves its bound reader and guarantees
form disposal in finally, including on failed assertions. Callback fixtures use
the same disposal guarantee.

The separate root-owned native event-loop check retains normal blocking
ShowDialog, production Paint/Activated handlers, repaint, activation,
minimize/restore, resizing and clean close. Its final-source evidence under
`../native-event-loop-20261005/` confirms no native thread exception and a
multicolored picture-only logo. No production runspace or logo-rendering change
was made to mask the fixture problem.

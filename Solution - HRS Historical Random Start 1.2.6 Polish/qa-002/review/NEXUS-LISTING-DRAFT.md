**A different beginning.**

Historical Random Start is a 7 Days to Die mod by Bit Wrecked. It gives a new
character a random starting location in the active world, then lets the normal
opening journey continue.

Choose any eligible biome, select a particular biome, or set custom weights.
Standard keeps the usual start. Optional starting-biome protection covers
recognized environmental hazards; enemies and hazards in other biomes remain
active. If no safe destination can be completed, HRS keeps the usual start.
A completed landing happens once, including across reloads and normal respawns.

**Get started**

Extract the complete ZIP to a writable local folder outside OneDrive or other
synced or linked folders. Close the game, keep Steam open, and run `START.bat`.
Enter the exact Game Name for your new save, choose your settings, then select
**Apply Settings**. Acknowledge **Settings Applied**, select **Launch Game** and
create the new game with that same name. The README covers upgrades and removal.

**Before leaving your first Random session**

Complete the opening tasks until the **Journey to Settlement trader marker**
appears. You can visit the trader later. Leaving earlier, including an unexpected
session end, may point the quest to a Pine Forest trader when you return. Your
landing stays saved and will not repeat. If you already left early, follow the
marker you receive on return. This is a known issue; the reminder does not repair
the route. A trader in your landing biome is preferred, but a nearby or same-biome
trader is not guaranteed.

**Compatibility**

Target: **7 Days to Die V3.3.0 b17, Windows, local single-player**, with
PowerShell 5.1, **EAC disabled** and the game's supplied **`0_TFP_Harmony`**.
Intended map scope: **Navezgane and Random Gen worlds of at least 8K**.
Bundled pregens and custom maps are outside the tested scope. Other game builds
are best-effort and need separate compatibility testing. HRS leaves the usual
start alone when the required hooks are incompatible.

This **Tech Demo** is a player tryout build. Further polish may follow.

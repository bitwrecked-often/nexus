# Historical Random Start 1.2.6 — Tech Demo

*A different beginning in 7 Days to Die. By Bit Wrecked.*

Historical Random Start gives a new character a random starting location in
the active world. Let the biome surprise you, choose one, or set custom weights.
Optional protection covers recognized environmental hazards in the starting
biome. Enemies and hazards in other biomes remain active.

## What's changed

The manager has a quieter layout, clearer setup instructions and distinct
Apply and Launch actions. Restore, uninstall and game details sit below the
main setup controls. Rounded light surfaces and outlined actions carry through
to the matching biome-weight editor. Standard mode
uses a compact window; Random reveals the biome choices and protection limits.
The header and window icons use the Bit Wrecked picture without lettering.
Apply and Launch use white buttons with subtle outlines. Small orange highlights
mark the release and Game details interaction. A short status line distinguishes
saved settings from verified readiness. Edited settings are marked
unapplied, Launch waits for matching verified settings, and the last-start
summary stays visible. **Restore previous settings** names its purpose clearly.

**Settings Applied** appears after the installation and selected Game Name,
biome choice and protection setting have been saved and verified. Random starts
include a reminder to complete the opening tasks in the first session until
**Journey to Settlement** assigns a trader. You can visit the trader later.
Standard starts omit that reminder.

Biome selection, one-shot landing, normal fallback and trader routing retain
the behavior from 1.2.5. The session reminder does not repair the trader issue.

## Before leaving your first Random session

**Complete the opening tasks until the Journey to Settlement trader marker
appears.** Known issue: leaving earlier, including an unexpected session end, may send the
quest to a Pine Forest trader when you return. Your landing stays saved and
will not repeat. If you already left early, follow the marker you receive on
return. You can visit the assigned trader in a later session.

## Compatibility and setup

The target is **7 Days to Die V3.3.0 b17, Windows, local single-player**,
with **anti-cheat (EAC) disabled** and the game's supplied **`0_TFP_Harmony`**.
The intended map scope is **Navezgane and Random Gen worlds of at least 8K**.
Bundled pregens and custom maps are outside the tested scope. Other game builds
are best-effort and have not been certified.

Extract the complete ZIP to a writable local folder outside OneDrive or other
synced or linked folders. Close the game, keep Steam open and run **`START.bat`**.
Enter the exact Game Name for a new save, apply settings, acknowledge the result
and launch. The README covers selection, upgrading, restoring and uninstalling.

This Tech Demo is a player tryout build. Further polish may follow.

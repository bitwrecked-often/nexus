# Known issue: trader quest after leaving a Random start

In Historical Random Start 1.2.5, leaving the world before finishing the starter tasks and opening **Journey to Settlement** trader visit may cause that quest to point to a Pine Forest trader when you return, even if your Random start landed in another biome. Your random landing remains saved and will not repeat.

For this release, finish the starter tasks and speak to the opening trader before leaving the world. If you have already left, you can still follow the quest marker to the trader it selected.

This affects the opening trader route in Random mode. Standard starts retain the game's normal route. We are tracking the logout-and-return behavior as a known issue.

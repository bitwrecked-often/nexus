# Known issue: trader quest after leaving a Random start

In Historical Random Start 1.2.5, leaving the world after a Random landing but before **Journey to Settlement** assigns a trader destination may cause that quest to point to a Pine Forest trader when you return, even if your Random start landed in another biome. Your random landing remains saved and will not repeat.

For this release, stay in the world until the trader destination appears. You can finish the trader visit later. If you have already left early, you can still follow the quest marker to the trader it selected.

This affects the opening trader route in Random mode. Standard starts retain the game's normal route. We are tracking the logout-and-return behavior as a known issue.

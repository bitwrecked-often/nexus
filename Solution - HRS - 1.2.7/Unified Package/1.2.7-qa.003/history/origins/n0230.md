## Admission rules

An anchor enters the selectable pool only after an in-game survey proves:

1. the resolved position is inside world and non-radiation bounds;
2. terrain, support, water, collision, and headroom checks pass;
3. the point is outside occupied prefab bounds;
4. the containing chunk loads and placement verifies;
5. the player is not trapped and has at least two practical exit directions;
6. removal of the mod leaves the world and vanilla spawn files unchanged.

NG05 is intentionally conditional. It is included because an island start is
interesting, but it must not enter the pool unless the live survey confirms a
dry, reachable landing area.
